# Acceptable Use Policy

**Product:** Fluenci — AI-Powered Language Learning Platform
**Company:** NovaWealth
**Effective Date:** 2026-04-23
**Policy Owner:** Tyler Moore
**Review Cycle:** Annual
**Contact:** security@fluenci.app

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines acceptable and prohibited uses of the Fluenci platform to protect users, institutional partners, and the integrity of the service. All users agree to this policy upon account creation.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all Fluenci users, including:

- **Learners** (students enrolled via institutional or individual accounts)
- **Teachers** (instructors managing classrooms and assignments)
- **School Administrators** (institutional staff managing organization accounts)
- **NovaWealth personnel** (engineering and support staff)

## 3. Permitted Use

Fluenci is provided exclusively for educational language learning purposes. Permitted activities include:

- Completing lessons, exercises, and review sessions.
- Participating in AI-powered conversational practice.
- Managing classrooms, assignments, and student progress (teachers).
- Administering organization accounts, provisioning users, and accessing audit logs (school admins).
- Using the platform in accordance with the subscribing institution's code of conduct.

## 4. Prohibited Activities

The following activities are strictly prohibited:

- **Harassment and abuse:** Using chat, conversation practice, or any platform feature to harass, bully, threaten, or discriminate against any person.
- **Credential sharing:** Sharing login credentials, magic links, or authentication tokens with any other person.
- **Security circumvention:** Attempting to bypass authentication, RLS policies, role restrictions, or any other security control.
- **Automated scraping:** Using bots, scripts, or automated tools to extract data, exercises, or content from the platform without authorization.
- **Unauthorized data storage:** Storing personal data of other users, exporting student data without authorization, or uploading content unrelated to language learning.
- **Reverse engineering:** Decompiling, disassembling, or reverse-engineering any part of the Fluenci application, API, or Edge Functions.
- **Prompt injection:** Attempting to manipulate, extract, or expose AI system prompts or model configurations.
- **Impersonation:** Misrepresenting identity, role, or organizational affiliation.

## 5. Content Standards

All user-generated content (conversation practice input, messages, profile information) must:

- Use appropriate language suitable for an educational environment.
- Contain no hate speech, slurs, sexually explicit material, or violent content.
- Comply with the subscribing institution's code of conduct and applicable laws.
- Not attempt to elicit inappropriate, harmful, or off-topic responses from AI features.

AI-generated content is filtered through a content safety pipeline before display. Users who encounter inappropriate AI output should report it immediately.

## 6. Account Responsibilities

- Users are responsible for all activity under their account.
- Passwords must meet minimum complexity requirements enforced at registration.
- Users must log out of shared or public devices after each session.
- Lost or compromised credentials must be reported to the institution's administrator or security@fluenci.app immediately.

## 7. Monitoring and Enforcement

- Fluenci logs administrative actions, authentication events, and security-relevant activities in an immutable audit log.
- NovaWealth reserves the right to monitor platform usage for security, performance, and policy compliance.
- Monitoring is conducted in accordance with applicable privacy laws and FERPA requirements.

## 8. Consequences of Violation

Violations of this policy may result in:

| Severity | Response |
|----------|----------|
| Minor (first offense, low impact) | Written warning to user and institutional admin |
| Moderate (repeated offense or policy circumvention) | Temporary account suspension; institutional admin notified |
| Severe (data breach attempt, harassment, security circumvention) | Immediate account termination; institutional admin and, if applicable, law enforcement notified |

NovaWealth cooperates with institutional disciplinary processes and applicable legal authorities.

## 9. Reporting Violations

Users who observe or suspect a policy violation should report it through one of the following channels:

- **Email:** security@fluenci.app
- **In-app:** Report feature (where available)
- **Institutional contact:** The user's school or organization administrator

Reports are acknowledged within 48 hours and investigated promptly. Retaliation against good-faith reporters is prohibited.
