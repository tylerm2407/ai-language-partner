# Fluenci — AI-Powered Language Learning for Higher Education

---

## The Problem

Language departments face growing enrollment with limited tutoring resources. Students need daily, personalized practice outside the classroom, but human tutors don't scale. Most apps offer passive recognition drills — not the active speaking and writing practice that builds fluency.

## The Solution

Fluenci provides AI-powered conversational tutoring, adaptive exercises, and teacher-managed assignments in one mobile app. Students practice speaking, listening, reading, and writing with an AI tutor that adapts to their CEFR level — giving every learner the equivalent of a personal tutor, available 24/7.

---

## Key Features

- **AI conversation practice** — Real-time voice dialogue adapted to the student's proficiency level and current unit
- **Adaptive exercises** — Recognition to production progression using SM-2 spaced repetition across 10+ exercise types
- **Teacher dashboard** — Create assignments, auto-grade with AI, manual override, view per-student progress
- **Classroom management** — Invite codes, bulk CSV enrollment, organization-scoped access control
- **Four-skill training** — Listening, speaking, reading, and writing in every course
- **CEFR-aligned progress tracking** — Students and teachers see proficiency trajectory over time
