# Fluenci — Teacher Quickstart

This guide covers everything you need to set up your classroom, assign work, and track student progress.

---

## 1. Getting Started

- Your school admin will create your teacher account during pilot setup.
- Log in at **app.fluenci.app** or in the mobile app using your **@school.edu email**.
- You'll land on the **Teacher Portal** — your hub for classrooms, assignments, and student data.

---

## 2. Creating a Classroom

1. Go to **Teacher Portal** → **Create Classroom**.
2. Configure your classroom:
   - **Name** — e.g., "Spanish 101 - Section A"
   - **Language** — the target language students will learn
   - **Level** — starting CEFR level (A1, A2, B1, etc.)
3. Click **Create**. You'll receive a unique **invite code**.
4. Share this code with your students so they can join.

You can create up to 3 classrooms during the pilot.

---

## 3. Enrolling Students

**Option A: Invite Code**
- Share the classroom invite code. Students enter it during sign-up or from their settings.

**Option B: CSV Bulk Import**
- Go to your classroom → **Enroll Students** → **Upload CSV**.
- CSV format: two columns — `email` and `name`.
- Students receive an email invitation to create their account.

---

## 4. Creating Assignments

1. Open your classroom → **Create Assignment**.
2. Fill in the details:
   - **Title** — e.g., "Week 3: Food & Drink Vocabulary"
   - **Mode** — Text only, Voice only, or Either
   - **Language & Level** — pre-filled from classroom settings (editable per assignment)
   - **Due Date** — when students must submit by
3. Click **Publish**. Students see it immediately in their Assignments tab.

Recommended pace: 1-3 assignments per week per classroom.

---

## 5. Grading

- **AI auto-grading:** Every submission is graded automatically with rubric-based feedback.
  - Single-word answers: exact match (accent-tolerant, case-insensitive)
  - Sentences: fuzzy match allowing minor typos
  - Free production: AI evaluates for meaning, grammar, and level-appropriateness
- **Your review:** Open any submission to see the AI score and feedback.
  - **Override the score** if you disagree with the AI grade.
  - **Add your own feedback** for the student.
- **Submission view:** See all student submissions for an assignment in one list, sorted by status.

---

## 6. Monitoring Progress

Your **Dashboard** gives you a real-time view of your classroom:

- **Student Activity** — Who's practicing, how often, and for how long
- **Completion Rates** — Percentage of assignments submitted per student
- **Average Scores** — Class-wide and per-student performance
- **CEFR Progress** — Track each student's movement through proficiency levels
- **Engagement Alerts** — Students who haven't logged in recently

Use this data to identify students who need extra support or are ready for more challenge.

---

## 7. Managing Your Class

From your classroom settings, you can:

- **View roster** — See all enrolled students and their status
- **Remove students** — Remove a student from your classroom (their account remains active)
- **Deactivate classroom** — Archive a classroom at the end of a term (data is preserved)

---

## 8. Admin Features

If your school admin has given you the `school_admin` role, you also have access to:

- **Manage Teachers** — Add or remove teacher accounts
- **Audit Logs** — View a log of all administrative actions
- **Data Export** — Export organization-level usage data
- **Data Deletion** — Request deletion of student or organization data (FERPA-compliant)

---

## 9. Support

During the pilot, you have direct access to the Fluenci team:

- **Weekly check-ins** — Scheduled during the first month, then biweekly
- **Dedicated support channel** — Slack or email thread for bug reports, questions, and feature requests
- **Response times:**
  - Critical issues (service down): 1 hour
  - Major issues (feature broken): 4 hours
  - Minor issues and questions: 24-48 hours
- **Email:** support@fluenci.app
