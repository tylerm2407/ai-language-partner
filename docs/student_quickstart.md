# Fluenci — Student Quickstart

Welcome to Fluenci! This guide will get you up and running in under 2 minutes.

---

## 1. Getting Started

- Download **Fluenci** from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com) or [Google Play](https://play.google.com).
- Sign up with your **@school.edu email**.
- Enter the **invite code** your teacher gave you. This connects you to your classroom.

---

## 2. Your First Lesson

- Tap **"Learn"** on the bottom menu.
- Pick a lesson from your current unit.
- Complete 10-15 short exercises. They start easy and get harder as you go.
- At the end, you'll see your results: items learned, accuracy, and XP earned.

---

## 3. Practice Types

You'll see a mix of these exercises in every lesson:

- **Multiple choice** — Pick the correct translation from four options.
- **Listening** — Hear a word or sentence and select or type what you heard.
- **Translation** — Type the translation in either direction (your language or the target language).
- **Speaking** — Record yourself saying a word or phrase. Fluenci scores your pronunciation.
- **Free writing** — Have an open conversation with the AI on a topic you're learning.

If you get stuck, hints are always available. If you get something wrong, the correct answer shows up right away so you can learn from it.

---

## 4. Daily Practice

- Aim for **5 minutes a day**. That's all it takes.
- Keep your **streak** alive by practicing every day.
- Earn **XP** for completing lessons, reviews, and practice sessions.

---

## 5. Assignments

- Check the **"Assignments"** section regularly.
- Your teacher may assign specific practice (text, voice, or both).
- Each assignment has a **due date**. Submit before it passes.

---

## 6. AI Tutor

- Tap **"Chat"** to start a conversation with the AI tutor.
- It adapts to your level — beginners get simpler vocabulary, advanced learners get more challenge.
- The AI gives corrections as you go, not just at the end.
- Great for practicing real conversation skills.

---

## 7. Review

- Missed items automatically go into your **review queue**.
- Fluenci uses spaced repetition — it brings back words right when you're about to forget them.
- The more you review, the longer you remember.

---

## 8. Need Help?

- Ask your teacher first.
- Email **support@fluenci.app** for technical issues.

Happy learning!
