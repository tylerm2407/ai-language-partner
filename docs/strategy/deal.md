# Fluenci x Bryant University — Deal Structure

*Prepared for IT & Language Department meeting — April 24, 2026*

---

## The Opportunity

Bryant's Department of Communication and Language Studies offers four languages (Spanish, French, Italian, Chinese) with limited faculty bandwidth for individualized practice. Fluenci provides AI-powered conversational tutoring, adaptive exercises, pronunciation coaching, and writing evaluation — supplementing what instructors do in class and extending practice into the hours between meetings.

Fluenci also supports languages Bryant doesn't currently teach (German, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Russian), giving students access to additional languages without hiring new faculty.

---

## Proposed Deal: Founding Partner Agreement

### Structure: Infrastructure Cost-Share + Co-Development

Bryant University becomes Fluenci's **Founding Institutional Partner**. The deal is designed so Bryant assumes minimal financial risk while gaining a custom-configured platform and long-term pricing advantages.

---

### Phase 1: Pilot Semester (Fall 2026)

**Scope:**
- 2-4 language course sections (50-150 students)
- 1-2 faculty champions from the Language Studies department
- Full platform access: lessons, AI conversation, writing lab, reading library, pronunciation scoring, SRS review

**Cost to Bryant: $0**
- No licensing fees during the pilot
- NovaWealth absorbs all infrastructure and AI costs during this phase
- Zero financial risk for the university

**Bryant provides:**
- Access to students and faculty for the pilot
- Faculty input on curriculum alignment (which units/vocabulary match their syllabi)
- A brief student satisfaction survey at semester end
- Permission to use "Piloted at Bryant University" in future sales materials

**Success metrics (agreed upfront):**
- Student engagement: ≥60% of enrolled students use the platform at least 3x/week
- Learning outcomes: measurable improvement on a pre/post vocabulary or proficiency assessment
- Faculty satisfaction: instructor(s) would recommend continuing use
- Technical reliability: <1% downtime, <24hr support response time

**Decision deadline:** Pilot agreement includes a decision date (December 2026) to convert or end. No open-ended evaluation.

---

### Phase 2: Institutional Deployment (Spring 2027+)

If the pilot meets success criteria, Bryant converts to a paid agreement:

**Pricing model — AI Cost Pass-Through:**

| Component | Cost | Notes |
|-----------|------|-------|
| Software license | **$0** | Free for Bryant as Founding Partner — permanent |
| AI usage costs | **Pass-through at cost** | Actual API charges (Claude, Gemini, Whisper, TTS) billed monthly with transparent invoicing |
| Estimated per-student cost | **$2-5/student/semester** | Based on moderate usage (15 min/day average); scales with actual consumption |
| Hosting & infrastructure | **Included** | Absorbed by NovaWealth |

**Why this works for Bryant:**
- Per-student cost is dramatically lower than any competitor ($10-30/student/year is industry standard)
- Transparent billing — Bryant sees exactly what they're paying for (API call logs)
- No long-term commitment — costs scale linearly with usage; if students stop using it, costs drop to zero
- No shelf-ware risk

**Why this works for NovaWealth:**
- We get a live institutional deployment and case study
- AI costs are covered so we're not burning cash on usage
- The reference customer and data are worth more than licensing revenue at this stage

## Security & Compliance

### Current State
- FERPA-compliant data handling: no student data shared with third parties; data retained only for educational purposes
- All data encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256)
- Hosted on Supabase (built on AWS infrastructure)
- Content safety filtering on all AI-generated output
- Role-based access control (student, teacher, admin)
- Audit logging for all administrative actions
- Privacy policy, terms of service, and data processing agreement drafted

### What We Need (and how Bryant can help)

| Requirement | Status | Proposal |
|-------------|--------|----------|
| **HECVAT** | Not yet completed | We will complete the HECVAT assessment before pilot launch. HECVAT is free and does not require a formal audit — it's a self-assessment questionnaire. We can reference AWS's publicly available HECVAT responses for infrastructure questions. |
| **SOC 2 Type II** | Not completed | Not required for a pilot. If Bryant requires it for a full deployment, we propose Bryant funds the audit (~$10,000-30,000) in exchange for an extended free licensing period or permanent Founding Partner pricing. |
| **VPAT (Accessibility)** | In progress | We will deliver a completed VPAT documenting WCAG 2.1 AA compliance status before pilot launch. |
| **Penetration test** | Not completed | Same proposal as SOC 2 — if required, Bryant funds the test; we fix any findings and Bryant benefits from the hardened platform at no software cost. |

**The trade:** Bryant's investment in security validation ($10K-30K one-time) buys them permanent free licensing on a platform that would otherwise cost $10-30/student/year. At 500 language students, that's $5,000-15,000/year in avoided licensing costs — the security investment pays for itself in 1-2 years.

## Why This Deal Makes Sense for Bryant IT

**1. Near-zero financial risk.**
The pilot costs nothing. Post-pilot, Bryant pays only actual AI usage — no minimum commitments, no multi-year lock-in.

**2. Student-built, student-focused.**
Tyler and Owen are Bryant students. This is Bryant's own ecosystem producing software that serves Bryant's academic mission. Supporting student entrepreneurs aligns with the Sprague Center's mandate.

**3. Better than the free alternative.**
Duolingo for Schools is free but offers no institutional analytics, no LMS integration, no faculty control over content, no writing evaluation, no voice conversation practice, and no classroom assignment system. Fluenci provides all of these.

**4. Fills a real gap.**
Bryant offers 4 languages. Students interested in Korean, Japanese, German, Portuguese, or Russian have no Bryant-supported option. Fluenci immediately expands language access without hiring new faculty.

**5. The security trade is a good deal.**
$10-30K for a security audit that produces a permanently free platform is a fraction of what Bryant would pay for any commercial alternative over 3-5 years.
